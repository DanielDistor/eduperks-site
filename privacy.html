Privacy Policy

Last updated October 25, 2026

Information We Collect

Your school email address, used to sign you in with a one-time code and to identify your school. The perks you save or mark as using (and any renewal date you set), your "worked / didn't work" feedback on perks, and the perk links you suggest. If you choose to answer: how you heard about EduPerks, your graduation year and your interests. If you agreed to emails about new perks in an earlier version of the app, we keep that answer and the date. Basic in-app activity, such as when you open the app, which perks you view and open, and what you search for, linked to your account so we can improve the app. Search text is deleted after 90 days and other activity after 13 months. Your phone type (iPhone or Android), the app version and your language setting. Before you sign in, we count steps of the sign-up screens and your school's web domain, without anything that identifies you. Crash and error reports, which contain technical details about your device and the app but not your email or name. We do not collect your name, phone number, location, contacts or payment information.

How We Use Your Information

To verify that you are a student, show you perks for your school and country, keep your saved perks in sync across devices, and fix problems in the app. We only email you about new perks if you agreed to that, and every email has a link to stop them. We do not show ads.

Service Providers

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We use a few providers to run the app: Supabase stores your account and data, Resend delivers our sign-in and perk emails, and Sentry receives crash reports. To find deals, we send school names and web domains (never your email or anything about you) to web search services (Firecrawl, Tavily, Exa and Anthropic Claude) and to Google Gemini and Claude to read the results. Perk logos are loaded from third-party image servers such as Google, which can see your device's IP address when an image loads.

Deleting Your Account

You can delete your account at any time in Profile → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, profile and answers, saved and in-use perks, feedback, activity history, and school and perk suggestions, and clears the app's data on your device. Crash reports are kept by Sentry for up to 90 days and are not linked to your email. If you can't access the app, email support@eduperks.app from your school address and we will delete your account for you.

Data Retention

We keep your data while you have an account and delete it when you delete your account.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and each user can only access their own data. Sign-in uses one-time codes, so we never store passwords.

Where Your Data Is Stored

Your account and data are stored by Supabase on servers in South Korea. Resend and Sentry process the data they receive in the United States. This means your data may be stored and processed outside your country. We only use providers that protect it with encryption and contracts that limit what they can do with it.

Your Rights

Wherever you live, you can see the data we hold about you, correct it, get a copy of it, withdraw your consent to perk emails, and delete your account. Most of this is in Profile. For anything else, email support@eduperks.app and we will reply within 30 days.

If you are in the Philippines, we process your personal information under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), with your consent when you sign up. You have the right to be informed, to access, to object, to correct, to erase or block, to data portability, and to claim damages. If you believe we have not respected these rights, you can file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

If you are in California or another US state with a privacy law, you have the same rights to know, correct and delete your data. We do not sell or share your personal information for advertising.

Children's Privacy

EduPerks is intended for students aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to Terms & Policy

We may update these Terms or Privacy Policy at any time. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance. We will notify you of significant changes through the app.

Contact

For any questions about these terms or your data, contact us at support@eduperks.app.
