How can we help?

EduPerks finds the student discounts and free perks your school email unlocks.

Contact us

Questions, a perk that didn't work, or a problem with the app: email us and we'll get back to you.

Email support@eduperks.app

I'm not getting my sign-in code

Check your spam or junk folder, and make sure you typed your school email correctly. Codes expire after an hour. If lots of students are signing up at once, the app tells you when to try again.

A perk didn't work

Open the perk and tap “Didn't work”. Perks that several students report are hidden automatically, and we recheck every link regularly.

Delete your account

In the app, go to Settings → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account and data. If you can't get into the app, email support@eduperks.app from your school address and we'll delete it for you.
